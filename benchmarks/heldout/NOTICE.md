# Data notices

- `blocked_in_frame.txt` is derived from the UT1 blacklists (`adult`, `mixed_adult`,
  `gambling`, `arjel`), Université Toulouse Capitole, https://dsi.ut-capitole.fr/blacklists/,
  licence CC BY-SA 4.0 (as stated by the mirror at https://github.com/olbat/ut1-blacklists).
  It lists the hosts of the frame that those files mark, and is shared under the same licence.
- `sample.json` draws from the Chrome UX Report (CrUX), September 2026, a dataset by Google
  under CC BY 4.0, as cached by https://github.com/zakird/crux-top-lists.
- `public_suffix_list.dat` is the Public Suffix List, unchanged, from
  https://publicsuffix.org/list/public_suffix_list.dat, licence MPL 2.0 (stated at the top of the file).
